🎬 Video 1 — Subject and Object Pronouns
they – them · she – her · he – him

Grammar Notes

Subject pronouns do the action: I, you, he, she, it, we, they.

Object pronouns receive the action: me, you, him, her, it, us, them.

1. Translate into English

Сара оларға көмектеседі.
💡 Hint: they → them

2. Translate into English

Том оған құжат береді.
💡 Hint: she → her

3. Translate into English

Олар оны тыңдайды.
💡 Hint: he → him
🎬 Video 2 — Question Words
What? · Who? · Where?

Grammar Notes

What? — Не?   Who? — Кім?   Where? — Қай жерде?

In Present Simple questions with does, use the base form of the main verb.

1. Translate the question and answer

Әже не жасайды?
Ол бауырсақ жасайды.
💡 Hint: What?

2. Translate the question and answer

Әжеге кім көмектеседі?
Оған немересі көмектеседі.
💡 Hint: Who?

3. Translate the question and answer

Отбасы бауырсақты қай жерде жейді?
Олар бауырсақты дастарқан басында жейді.
💡 Hint: Where?
🎬 Video 3 — Have to / Don’t have to
School rules and routines

Grammar Notes

have to = міндетті / істеу керек.

don’t have to = міндетті емес; it does not mean “must not”.

1. Translate into English

Біз мектеп формасын киюіміз керек.
💡 Hint: school clothes

2. Translate into English

Біз сенбі күні мектепке баруға міндетті емеспіз.
💡 Hint: Saturday · school
🏆 4. Final Mission — My Future Profession

Your mission: Write a short story of 4–5 sentences about a profession you like (for example, a teacher, doctor, engineer, or chef). Use the grammar you learned in this module.

✍️ My Future Profession

Write 4–5 sentences in English. Try to include:

  • a subject or object pronoun (he, she, him, her, them…)
  • a question word (what, who, or where) in a question
  • have to to describe a duty
  • don’t have to to describe something that is not necessary
Helpful words: help people · wear a uniform · work at a hospital/school · follow rules · work on weekends
